How an engagement works

Map the work as it happens today

We sit with the people doing the job and document the real workflow, including the workarounds, the copy-paste, and the "someone just remembers to do it" steps.

Automate the steps that don't need a person

Data entry, routing, status checks, reminders, reports. We connect the tools you already pay for and add AI where judgment calls can be drafted for a human to approve.

Hand it back, documented and owned by you

You get the system, the documentation, and a plain-language runbook. Nothing is locked to us, and your team can see exactly what runs and when.

Measure what changed

Every build starts with a baseline: hours per week, error rate, response time. We report against that number so you know what the work actually returned.

What we build

  • Intake and routingEmails, forms, calls, and messages captured, classified, and sent to the right queue with the details already filled in.
  • Status tracking and follow-upOpen items that chase themselves: authorizations, invoices, applications, tickets. Reminders go out and escalate without anyone keeping a list.
  • Reporting and dashboardsThe numbers you check every morning pulled automatically from your systems and delivered where you already look.
  • AI assistants for repetitive judgmentDrafting replies, summarizing records, flagging exceptions. The assistant prepares the work; a person approves it.
  • System-to-system connectionsGetting data from one platform into another without re-keying, whether the tools have an API or not.

Built by someone who runs operations, not just software

Woptimize was started by Steven Eisenberger, who manages day-to-day operations for a group of healthcare practices: billing, payroll, vendors, IT, and staff. The automations we build are the ones we built first for ourselves, because the manual version was eating the team's week.

That means we know what it is like to introduce a new system to a busy staff, and why the ones that survive are the ones that fit the existing routine.
